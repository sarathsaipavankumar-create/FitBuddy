🔍 Why BMI is Only a Screening Measure

BMI is strictly an initial population screening tool, not a diagnostic measure of body composition. It calculates a simple mathematical ratio between your height and total weight. Because it cannot distinguish between heavy, dense muscle tissue and body fat, a lean muscular athlete can register in the "Overweight" range while an untrained individual with low muscle mass may register as "Normal".

⚠️ Not Sufficient for "Bulk vs. Cut" Decisions: A single BMI value by itself is not sufficient to decide whether you should bulk (eat in a caloric surplus to add mass) or cut (eat in a caloric deficit to reduce fat). Such decisions require considering your actual body composition, waist measurements, daily strength progression, and energy levels.

* Important Age Restriction Note: Standard adult BMI classifications apply only to men and women aged 20 and older. Adult BMI cutoffs are not intended for children and adolescents, whose growth must be interpreted using age-and-sex-specific pediatric percentiles.
